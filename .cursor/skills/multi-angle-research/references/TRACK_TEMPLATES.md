# Multi-Angle Research — Track Templates

Full markdown templates for each output. Use these when writing the files.

---

## Track 4 Template — Devil's Advocate

```markdown
# Track 4: Devil's Advocate — Why [Company] Should NOT Pursue [Topic]

## The Central Argument Against
[1–2 paragraph setup]

## Pitfall 1: [Name]
## Pitfall 2: [Name]
...
## Pitfall N: [Name]

## Summary: The Risk Matrix
| Risk Category | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Difficulty |
|---------------|----------|------------|------------------------|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Conclusion of the Devil's Advocate Case
[Honest summary — genuine caution, not dismissal]

## Key Sources
- [URL]
- [URL]
```

**Aim for 8–12 named pitfalls.** Each grounded in Track 1–3 evidence.

## Track 5 Template — Contrarian View & Recommendation

```markdown
# Track 5: Contrarian View — Should [Company] Actually Pursue [Topic]?

## The Contrarian Position in Brief
[1–2 sentence thesis — the "third path"]

## Why the [Affirmative] Case Is Premature / Overstated
## Why the [Defensive] Case Misses Something Important
## The Structural Argument (what the mainstream narrative gets wrong)
## Evidence to Look For Before Committing
## A Specific Recommendation
[Concrete, action-oriented]
- **Scope:** [how narrow/broad]
- **Timeline:** [time-boxed pilot vs full commitment]
- **Success criteria:** [defined in advance, measurable]
- **Learning value:** [what it generates regardless of outcome]
- **Boundaries:** [what it does NOT commit to]

## The Honest Bottom Line
## Summary Table: What to Do and What to Avoid

## Sources
```

## Executive Summary Template

```markdown
# [Topic] — Executive Summary
**[Subtitle]** | [Date] | [Prepared for]

---

## 1. [Section title — e.g. The Business Case]
[3–4 sentences: affirmative case + what a pilot would generate]

---

## 2. [Section title — e.g. Competitive Landscape]
[3–4 sentences: key movers, notable non-movers, key insight]

---

## 3. [Section title — e.g. Platform Developments]
[3–4 sentences: what key platforms signal and why it matters]

---

## 4. [Section title — e.g. The Honest Risks]
- [Bullet 1]
- [Bullet 2]
- [Bullet 3]
- [Bullet 4–6]

---

## Recommendation

> [1-sentence contrarian thesis — the "third path"]

**[Why the affirmative case is premature/overstated — 2 sentences]**

**[Why the defensive case misses something — 2 sentences]**

**[Specific recommendation — scope, timeline, success criteria]**

**[Immediate actions that don't require the full pilot]**

*[Overarching insight — one sentence that reframes everything]*

---

*Sources: [brief citation note]*
```

**Target:** ~400–600 words. No nested headers inside sections.
